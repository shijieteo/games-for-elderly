    A good look, kaki?

    1. Choose a scene and find 3 or 5 differences between pictures A and B. Three-difference puzzles use a selection of the changes, so the other details really do match.
    2. Tap a changed detail in either picture. A numbered ring marks it in both pictures. There are no penalties for trying.
    3. Need a clue? A little hint highlights a grid square and gives you something to compare. Press it again for another clue. Show answers marks the remaining differences, labelled separately from those you found.
    4. For a big group, use Group view. It enlarges the pictures and hides extra page content. Call out coordinates such as “C2”. Press Escape or Exit group view to return.
    5. With a keyboard, Tab to either picture. Arrow keys move between squares; Home and End reach the first and last squares. Enter or Space checks the selected square for a difference. The text descriptions in the discoveries list explain each answer.

    There is no time limit. Change the scene or challenge to begin a fresh puzzle, or press Next scene to keep exploring.
