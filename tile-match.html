Let's play, kaki!

  1. Choose a theme, the number of tiles, and solo or team play. All pictures stay face up so you can remember them. Press Start game when you are ready to hide them.
  2. Tap a tile to reveal its picture. Tap another to look for the same picture. Each tile back has a number, so you can also call out the numbers for someone to tap.
  3. Matching tiles stay face up. If they are different, try again!
  4. Find all the pairs. Use Take a little peek whenever you need a hint.

Playing in teams: Team 1 goes first. Each team chooses two tiles and earns 1 point for a matching pair. Teams alternate after every guess, even a match. If the pictures differ, wait for them to hide before the next team plays. The team with the most pairs wins; equal scores are a tie.

There is no time limit and no penalty for taking a peek. With a keyboard, use Tab to reach a tile and Enter or Space to turn it over. Arrow keys also move between tiles.
