    A little colour, kaki?

    1. Choose 2–8 colours, select a playback pace and press Start game. Watch the big display: each colour has its own name and shape. Press Start fresh to change the colour count during play.
    2. When you see Your turn, tap the coloured buttons in the same order. You can also use the number keys shown on your buttons (up to 1–8), or use Tab and Enter or Space.
    3. Get the sequence right, then press Next round. The same sequence grows by one colour.
    4. Need a clue? Press A little hint to rule out wrong colours for the current step. With 3–8 colours, two choices remain; with 2 colours, only the answer remains. Each new step has a fresh hint available.
    5. Need another look? Press Watch again to replay from the beginning. If you make a mistake, you can try the same sequence again.

    Sound is optional. Each colour has a different note. Your best is the longest sequence you have correctly repeated, even if you used hints or replays. There is no time limit.
